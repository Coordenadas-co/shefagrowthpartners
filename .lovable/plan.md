

# Add Business Logo

## What
Replace the text-only "Shefa Growth Partners" branding in the Navbar and Footer with the uploaded gold tree logo image.

## Steps

1. **Copy logo** to `src/assets/logo.png`
2. **Update `src/components/Navbar.tsx`** — replace the text logo with an `<img>` tag (h-8/h-10) next to the "Shefa Growth Partners" text, or logo only depending on size
3. **Update `src/components/Footer.tsx`** — same treatment, add logo image next to or above the brand name

## Design
- Logo displayed at ~32-40px height in navbar, slightly larger in footer
- Keep the "Shefa Growth Partners" text alongside the logo for brand recognition
- Logo naturally fits the gold + navy theme
